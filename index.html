About ARYAN

ARYAN is a leading brand of premium stainless steel kitchen sinks, cisterns, vanity cabinets, and unbreakable home accessories. With state-of-the-art manufacturing and an unwavering commitment to quality, we serve homes and businesses across India.

Our kitchen sink range spans 6 distinct series from our finest Handmade collection to the value-driven Economy line, with Handmade available in both 304 Grade and 202 Grade Stainless Steel.
